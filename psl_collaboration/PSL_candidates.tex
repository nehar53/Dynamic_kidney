\subsection*{How this list was checked}

Universit\'e Paris Dauphine-PSL, LAMSADE, CNRS). The official pages themselves could
not be opened directly from our environment, so every entry should be confirmed by
opening the profile link before contact. Items that could not be confirmed are marked
\textbf{[unverified]}.

\subsection*{1. Olivier Tercieux}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Position:} CNRS research director (directeur de recherche); chaired
        professor at the Paris School of Economics (PSE); affiliated researcher at the
        Institut des politiques publiques (IPP).
  \item \textbf{PSL institution:} PSE is a foundation whose founding members include
        ENS-PSL and CNRS. His research unit, Paris Jourdan Sciences \'Economiques
        (UMR 8545), lists ENS-PSL among its supervising bodies, and the national registry
        (RNSR) lists PSL. Whether he can act as the PSL sponsor of a GSF application
        through ENS-PSL is \textbf{[unverified: confirm with PSL International Relations or
        ENS-PSL]}.
  \item \textbf{Research focus:} mechanism design, matching, game theory, education.
  \item \textbf{Alignment:} closest to our work. Co-author of ``Unpaired Kidney Exchange:
        Overcoming Double Coincidence of Wants without Money'' (Review of Economic
        Studies, 2025), evaluated with French patient data, and of a study of the French
        paired kidney donation programme using data from the Agence de la biom\'edecine.
        Fits our myopic vs batched results and dynamic matching questions.
  \item \textbf{Profile:} \url{https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/en/people/olivier-tercieux}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{2. Laurent Gourv\`es}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Position:} CNRS research director (since 2019); adjunct professor at PSL
        University (since September 2022); former head of LAMSADE's Optimization,
        Structure and Algorithms department (2020--2024).
  \item \textbf{PSL institution:} LAMSADE (CNRS UMR 7243), Universit\'e Paris Dauphine-PSL.
  \item \textbf{Research focus:} combinatorial optimisation, approximation, algorithmic
        game theory, decision, social choice.
  \item \textbf{Alignment:} fair and efficient allocation (recent work on fair budget
        apportionment); combinatorial structure of matching and packing problems. Fits
        the fairness criteria and the cycle/chain packing model. No kidney exchange paper
        found \textbf{[unverified]}.
  \item \textbf{Profile:} \url{https://dauphine.psl.eu/en/research/resume-database/profile/gourves-laurent}
        and \url{https://lamsade.dauphine.fr/~gourves}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{3. Daniel Vanderpooten}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Position:} Professor, Universit\'e Paris Dauphine-PSL; listed as head of
        the Master MODO. Current title on the Dauphine CV database \textbf{[unverified:
        no CV database profile found]}.
  \item \textbf{PSL institution:} LAMSADE, Universit\'e Paris Dauphine-PSL.
  \item \textbf{Research focus:} multi-objective combinatorial optimisation and
        multicriteria decision aid; exact computation and representation of the
        nondominated set.
  \item \textbf{Alignment:} exact or approximate Pareto fronts to benchmark our
        evolutionary front below maximum volume (a point raised by our two-stage ILP
        results); preference-based selection of one solution from the front.
  \item \textbf{Profile:} \url{https://lamsade.dauphine.fr/en/people/faculty.html} and
        publication list \url{https://www.lamsade.dauphine.fr/~vdp/publi_vdp.html}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{4. J\'er\^ome Lang}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Position:} CNRS senior researcher (directeur de recherche) at LAMSADE;
        CNRS silver medal (2017); Humboldt Research Award (2021); co-editor of the
        Handbook of Computational Social Choice (2016).
  \item \textbf{PSL institution:} LAMSADE, Universit\'e Paris Dauphine-PSL.
  \item \textbf{Research focus:} computational social choice (voting, fair division,
        matching, participatory budgeting); knowledge representation and reasoning.
  \item \textbf{Alignment:} axiomatic view of our fairness criteria and selection rule;
        fairness over time in repeated allocation. No kidney exchange paper found
        \textbf{[unverified]}.
  \item \textbf{Profile:} \url{https://dauphine.psl.eu/en/research/resume-database/lang-jerome}
\end{itemize}
